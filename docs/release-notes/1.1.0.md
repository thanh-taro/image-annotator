# Image Annotator 1.1.0

Any colour and any size, without losing the quick choices.

## New
- **Custom colour.** After the 8 colours there is a ninth, custom swatch. Click it to open your browser's colour
  picker and choose any colour; the annotation changes as you move through colours. The swatch remembers your
  last custom colour, so you can give it to the next annotation with one click. It works for shapes, lines,
  arrows, the pen, the highlighter, text, steps and solid redaction. Step numbers and text outlines turn black
  or white, whichever reads better on your colour.
- **Custom size.** Next to Small, Medium and Large there is a size in pixels, with a slider on wider screens. It
  sets the line width of shapes, lines, arrows and the pen, the width of the highlighter, and the size of text
  and steps. The field always shows the current size; type a number or drag the slider for an exact size, or
  pick Small, Medium or Large to go back to sizes that follow the image.
- One pick in the colour picker, or one slider drag, is one undo step.

## Fixed
- Clicking the tool that is already active on a wide screen now clears the selection, as picking another tool
  does, so a style choice changes only what you draw next.
- Esc in an unchanged field of the style panel works as it does elsewhere (it clears the selection), and focus
  moves back to the image.

## Notes
- The canvas background keeps its 9 choices; it has no custom colour.
- Blur and pixelate strength stay Small, Medium and Large.
