# Image Annotator 1.2.1

## Changed
- **Fill colour is separate from the line colour.** When you turn on the fill of a rectangle or ellipse, the fill
  starts in the line colour; after that, changing the line colour no longer changes the fill. Pick a colour in
  Fill color to change the fill. Turning the fill off and on again keeps its colour.
- Turning on the fill for several shapes at once gives each its own line colour as its fill, in one undo step.

## Notes
- The text background is unchanged: until you pick a Background color it stays black or white, whichever reads
  better with the text colour.
