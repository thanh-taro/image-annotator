# Image Annotator 1.2.0

Filled shapes in any colour, and text that stands out on busy screenshots.

## New
- **Fill colour.** When a rectangle or ellipse is filled (Solid or Translucent), a Fill color row appears with
  the 8 colours and the custom swatch. Until you pick one, the fill follows the outline colour, as before; once
  you pick one, it stays when you change the outline. Example: a red outline around a translucent white fill.
- **Text background.** Text has a new Text background choice: None, Solid or Translucent. The text then sits on
  a box with rounded corners and a small margin, and its outline is dropped. Until you pick a Background color,
  the box is black or white, whichever reads better with the text colour. Clicking anywhere on the box selects
  the text, and the box moves, copies and deletes with it.
- Fill and background are remembered per tool (rectangle, ellipse, text), like sizes. Changing them on a
  selection is one undo step. Your custom colour is shared by the colour, fill colour and background colour rows.

## Changed
- Clicking a colour that is already marked does nothing (before, it could reset the shared drawing colour).

## Notes
- A Translucent text background is 70 % opaque, so the text stays readable; a Translucent shape fill stays 30 %.
- Margin and corner roundness are fixed; shapes always keep their outline.
