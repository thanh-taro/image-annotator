# Principles

- **PRN-01** — *Private by construction.* Images and annotations never leave the device: no
  uploads, no analytics, no share links, nothing stored between sessions. When in doubt, don't send.
- **PRN-02** — *Hidden means gone.* What the user redacts can't be recovered from the export.
  Effects that only obscure (blur, pixelate) say so honestly.
- **PRN-03** — *Seconds, not minutes.* Paste, mark, copy. Every common action is one gesture or
  one key; good defaults mean most annotations need no styling.
- **PRN-04** — *Never destructive until export.* The original image is never changed;
  annotations, crop and padding stay editable and undoable until the user exports.
- **PRN-05** — *Fits the host.* Looks and behaves like part of the portal when embedded, and
  remains fully usable on its own.
