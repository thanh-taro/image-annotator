# Personas

### PER-01 — Screenshot sharer (assumed)
A developer, QA engineer or technical writer using the taro-kit tools portal, often on Windows,
Linux, ChromeOS or a managed laptop where installing software is hard. Several times a week
they annotate a screenshot for a bug report, a ticket, a how-to guide, design feedback, or a
chat message, and often need to hide emails, tokens, names or customer data first. They want it
done in seconds, from the keyboard, and they don't trust tools that upload images.

### PER-02 — Portal host (owner)
The taro-kit portal that embeds the tool and its owner. Needs the tool to look and behave like
part of the portal (theme, language, sizing), to be embeddable only by trusted origins, and to
send the portal nothing but a ready signal.
