# Roadmap

### M-1 — Full annotator
Load by paste, drop or file; zoom and pan; rectangle, ellipse, line, arrow, pen, highlight,
text, numbered steps; solid, blur and pixelate redaction; select and edit every annotation;
undo/redo; non-destructive crop; padding and background; copy and download as metadata-free PNG;
keyboard map; leave-page warning; host integration, privacy statement, offline use and the
portal's language. Features: F-001 … F-018. Scope chosen by the owner (D-001).

### M-2 — Reach and polish
HEIC input; images beyond the RULE-02 limit without scaling down; JPEG and WebP export; style
presets; touch polish; text with background boxes; fixed crop ratios.

### M-3 — Candidates
Suggesting areas to redact using on-device text recognition; side-by-side comparison; templates
(device frames, shadows, rounded corners).
