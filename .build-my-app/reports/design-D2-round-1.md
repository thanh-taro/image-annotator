## Fixes
- **Sizes:** RULE-04 on the shorter side: stroke 7 px, text 44 px; the arrowhead scales with them (audit blocker).
- **Text outline:** RULE-08, 4 px white, drawn behind the fill (audit major).
- **Solid-redaction selection:** now has all four actions, on D2 and on the D1 StylePanel board (audit major). The D1 blur selection gets the same.
- **Canvas layout:** the flow is one column (audit major).
- **Annotation list:** a hidden list in drawing order, one item per annotation, with the selected item marked. The canvas summary count matches the list (a11y B1, M2).
- **Step 3 editor:** a real editable field (contenteditable textbox) that hugs the text, with the caret at the end; focus is in the field (a11y B2, UI major 1).
- **DOM order:** the panel comes before the canvas, shown on the right with CSS order. A main landmark is added (a11y M1, m2).
- **Live messages:**
  - step 1: loaded
  - step 2: "Arrow added"
  - step 4: "Solid (secure) added"
  - step 5: the notice itself is the polite live region
  - an empty role=alert region for errors (a11y M3, M4; audit minor)
- **Focus ring:** no permanent ring on the work area; the focus location is stated in each label (UI major 2, a11y m3).
- **Image edge:** 1 px lineStrong outline (UI minor 3).
- **Position fields:** each label hugs its input, with a wider column gap; also on D1 (UI minor 5).
- **Steps 4–5:** the email redaction is selected, and the field labels moved up so the handles clear them (UI minor 6).
- **Palette:** `--ann-*` properties; titles follow the adapter form, and step 2's label explains the rectangle (audit minors).

## Recorded, not changed

- **a11y m4:** the summary doesn't include tool or selection. Those are announced through the live region and the list's selected state.
- **Step 2a (rectangle alone):** not added. The step 2 label now says the rectangle was drawn first; the same tool-stays behaviour shows with the arrow.
- **vi-VN for this region:** covered in D4 (plan row "vi-VN default").
